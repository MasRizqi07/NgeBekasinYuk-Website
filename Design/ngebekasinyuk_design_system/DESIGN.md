## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-trust, mobile-first C2C electronics and tech marketplace engineered specifically for the Indonesian market. Buying and selling used electronics (smartphones, laptops, GPUs, gaming consoles) demands a psychological balance: absolute transactional safety combined with approachable, frictionless accessibility.

### Design Philosophy & Aesthetic
- **Style Archetype:** Modern Clean Consumer Marketplace (inspired by modern refined primitives, precision fintech cues, and utility-driven clarity).
- **Core Pillars:**
  - **Verified Security (Rekber/Escrow):** UI architecture highlights escrow protection, hardware diagnostics, and warranty badges with institutional precision.
  - **Frictionless Velocity:** Rapid listing flows, quick photo carousels, sticky contextual filters, and a high-salience floating listing action.
  - **Radical Clarity:** Zero visual clutter. Whitespace, subtle borders, crisp micro-interactions, and tabular financial readouts keep buyer attention on item condition, testing benchmarks, and transparent pricing.

## Colors

The color system delivers high readability and intuitive semantic cues tailored for consumer commerce:

### Primary & Action (Trust Blue)
- **Primary (`#0F6FFF`)**: Primary navigation, checkout triggers, selected active tabs, verified store checks, and verified buyer interactions.
- **Primary Hover/Active (`#0A5BD6`)**: Interactive state feedback across desktop and touch press.
- **Primary Soft (`#EBF2FF`)**: Active surface backgrounds, chip selections, and contextual highlights.

### Escrow & Trust (Escrow Green)
- **Secondary / Success (`#00C48C`)**: Dana Rekber (Escrow Guarantee), successful functional diagnostic checks, positive seller ratings, and transaction completions.
- **Success Light (`#E6F9F3`)**: Background for escrow status badges and success states.

### Accent & Conversion (Jual / Listing Orange)
- **Accent / FAB (`#FF7A00`)**: Dedicated exclusively to seller generation, the central "+ Jual" floating CTA, flash deal ribbons, and instant cashout triggers.
- **Accent Hover (`#E66E00`)**: Interactive state for seller actions.
- **Accent Soft (`#FFF2E5`)**: Background for seller promos, listing upgrades, and commission waivers.

### System & Alert Semantics
- **Danger / Dispute (`#E5484D`)**: Complaint center, dispute initiation, hardware defect alerts, and failed diagnostic warnings. Danger Soft: `#FDE8E8`.
- **Warning (`#F5A524`)**: Pending seller response, COD safety reminders, warranty expiration warnings. Warning Soft: `#FEF6E7`.

### Neutral & Surfaces
- **Canvas / App Background (`#F7F8FA`)**: Soft cool-grey tone providing separation from white card modules.
- **Surface Card (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure white base for all product listings, detail drawers, and form cards.
- **Subtle Surface (`#EFF2F6`)**: Input fills, divider strips, skeleton loaders, and non-active tags.
- **Border (`#E3E8EF`)**: Crisp low-contrast boundary lines providing structural integrity.
- **Text Primary (`#1A1D23`)**: Deep obsidian for high contrast readability across price points and specs.
- **Text Secondary (`#5C6575`)**: Supporting metadata, seller location, timestamps, and specs.
- **Text Muted (`#8A94A6`)**: Placeholders, breadcrumbs, inactive icons, and disabled states.

## Typography

The type system relies on `Inter` across all touchpoints, prioritizing legible tech specs, hardware model numbers, and Indonesian Rupiah (IDR) currency expressions.

### Tabular Numerical Configuration
All price displays, battery health percentages, RAM/Storage metrics, serial checks, and countdown timers must enforce tabular numbers:
```css
font-feature-settings: 'tnum' on, 'cv05' on, 'cv11' on;
```
This guarantees alignment across product comparison columns, transaction invoices, and feed cards without horizontal jitter.

### Hierarchy Applications
- **Display:** Reserved for high-impact onboarding banners and promotional campaign headlines.
- **Headline:** Item titles, seller shop headers, and drawer titles. Item names in cards truncate at 2 lines max.
- **Body:** Hardware condition descriptions, chat messages, return policy details, and diagnostic breakdown logs.
- **Price (`price-lg`, `price-md`, `price-sm`):** Custom high-weight configurations paired with tight tracking to make price discovery immediate.

## Layout & Spacing

A mobile-first 4px/8px modular base rhythm powers layouts from compact 360px Android devices up to 1440px desktop displays.

### Form Factor Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 640px):**
  - **Columns:** 2 columns for marketplace card feeds; 1 column for forms/checkout.
  - **Margins:** 16px (`container-padding-mobile`).
  - **Gutters:** 12px (`gutter-mobile`).
  - **Bottom Navigation:** 64px fixed dock with safe-area padding and a center-anchored "+ Jual" floating button.
- **Tablet (640px - 1024px):**
  - **Columns:** 3 to 4 fluid columns for listing feeds.
  - **Margins:** 20px.
  - **Gutters:** 16px.
- **Desktop (> 1024px, Max Width: 1280px Centered):**
  - **Columns:** 12-column grid. Feed items span 3 columns (4 items per row).
  - **Margins:** 24px (`container-padding-desktop`).
  - **Gutters:** 20px (`gutter-desktop`).
  - **Layout Model:** Fixed sticky left sidebar for filters/categories, sticky seller contact rail on product details.

### Vertical Rhythms & Compact Density
Electronics buyers scan specifications rapidly. Space within cards is kept compact (`space-sm` between spec tags), while vertical spacing between distinct semantic blocks adheres strictly to `space-xl` (24px) or `space-2xl` (32px).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined hybrid approach: clean borders (`#E3E8EF`) paired with diffuse ambient shadows to maintain a tactile, uncluttered product surface.

### Elevation Levels
1. **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** `#F7F8FA` background. No shadow.
2. **Level 1 (Card & Modular Surface):**
   - Border: `1px solid #E3E8EF`
   - Shadow: `0 1px 2px 0 rgba(16, 24, 40, 0.06)`
   - Used for product grid cards, search filters, seller profile cards, and review modules.
3. **Level 2 (Hover & Interactive Cards):**
   - Border: `1px solid #D0D5DD`
   - Shadow: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(16, 24, 40, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(16, 24, 40, 0.04)`
   - Used for card hover states, active dropdown menus, and popovers.
4. **Level 3 (Sticky Headers & Navigation):**
   - Surface: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`
   - Border Bottom: `1px solid #E3E8EF`
   - Shadow: `0 2px 8px rgba(16, 24, 40, 0.04)`
5. **Level 4 (Floating CTAs & Drawers):**
   - Shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(16, 24, 40, 0.16), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(16, 24, 40, 0.08)`
   - Used for the center "+ Jual" floating trigger, filter bottom-sheets on mobile, and chat heads.
6. **Primary CTA Glow:**
   - Shadow: `0 4px 16px 0 rgba(15, 111, 255, 0.32)`
   - Used on primary action buttons ("Beli Langsung", "Ajukan Rekber") to guide conversion flow.

## Shapes

The interface balances precision and modern approachability through a curated corner-radius scale:

- **Radius `sm` (6px):** Form controls, interior spec tags, tiny status badges, verification check dots.
- **Radius `md` (10px):** Default input fields, standard buttons, listing thumbnails, and segmented controls.
- **Radius `lg` (14px):** Product cards, dialog modals, bottom-sheet containers, and banner tiles.
- **Radius `xl` (20px):** Floating bottom navigation bar and photo gallery preview containers.
- **Radius `full` (9999px):** Avatar rings, pill filters, category tags, counter bubbles, and the "+ Jual" FAB button.

- **Primary Action ("Beli Sekarang", "Ajukan Penawaran"):** Background `#0F6FFF`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `10px` (`md`), font weight `600`. Height: `44px` on mobile, `40px` on desktop. Shadow: `0 4px 16px 0 rgba(15, 111, 255, 0.32)`. Hover: `#0A5BD6`.
- **Secondary ("Nego", "Chat Penjual"):** Background `#EBF2FF`, text `#0F6FFF`, border none, radius `10px`. Hover: `#D6E4FF`.
- **Floating Listing CTA ("+ Jual"):** Positioned center-docked on mobile or bottom-right floating on desktop. Background `#FF7A00`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `9999px` (`full`), padding `12px 24px`. Shadow: `0 12px 32px rgba(255, 122, 0, 0.35)`. Lucide icon `Plus` (20px) aligned leading.
- **Destructive ("Batalkan Transaksi", "Komplain Rekber"):** Background `#FDE8E8`, text `#E5484D`, hover background `#FCD3D3`.

### 2. Product Listing Cards
- **Card Anatomy:**
  - Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E3E8EF`, radius `14px` (`lg`), overflow hidden.
  - **Media Area:** 1:1 aspect ratio with subtle zoom on hover. Top-left overlay: Badge "Rekber Aktif" (Green `#00C48C`, text white, radius `6px`). Top-right overlay: Wishlist heart toggle with frosted backdrop (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`).
  - **Body Content:**
    - Item Name: `headline-sm`, max 2 lines, `#1A1D23`.
    - Spec Highlights (e.g., "M1 / 8GB / 256GB" or "BH 88%"): Soft grey pill badge `#EFF2F6`, text `#5C6575`, radius `6px`, font size `11px`.
    - Price: `price-md`, `#1A1D23`, tabular numbers enabled (`Rp14.850.000`).
    - Seller Meta: 12px Lucide icon `MapPin` + City ("Jakarta Selatan") and Star rating ("4.9 (42)"), text `#8A94A6`.

### 3. Filter Chips & Segmented Controls
- **Inactive:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E3E8EF`, text `#5C6575`, radius `9999px`, padding `6px 14px`.
- **Active / Selected:** Background `#EBF2FF`, border `1px solid #0F6FFF`, text `#0F6FFF`, font weight `600`.
- **Condition Toggles:** Segmented pill bar (`Like New 99%`, `Mulus 95%`, `Lecet Pemakaian`) using `#EFF2F6` container track with `#FFFFFF` active thumb indicator.

### 4. Input Fields & Search Bar
